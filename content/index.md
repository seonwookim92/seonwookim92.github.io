## Research Interests

My primary research interest lies in the **convergence of Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity**. I aim to develop intelligent systems that can autonomously identify, analyze, and respond to sophisticated cyber threats. My key areas of focus include:

- **Intelligent Security Automation**: Leveraging **Agentic AI** and **Large Language Models (LLM)** to automate and enhance complex security tasks, such as autonomous penetration testing and intelligent digital forensics analysis.
    
- **AI for Cybersecurity (AI for Security)**: Applying advanced machine learning models, including **Adversarial Learning** and **Diffusion Models**, for proactive threat detection, malware analysis, and vulnerability assessment.
    
- **Security in Artificial Intelligence (Security in AI)**: Investigating the security and robustness of AI systems themselves, focusing on adversarial attacks against machine learning models and developing corresponding defense mechanisms.
