I am a Cyber Officer in the Republic of Korea (ROK) Army and an AI & Security Researcher.

A graduate of the Korea Military Academy, I have been serving in the military's cyber operations field since 2015, with a particular focus on Offensive Security. 

Additionally, I hold a Master of Science in Computer Science from the University of Colorado Boulder (2021-2023). During my master's, I conducted diverse research, including a Google Summer of Code (GSoC) project on preventing GNSS attacks with AI and a Samsung-funded project on reinforcement learning-based cloud scheduling.

My research interests lie at the intersection of AI and cybersecurity, focusing on Agentic AI, AI-driven security, adversarial machine learning, and Offensive Security.
